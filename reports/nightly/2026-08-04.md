#### Calculation Logic

Based on verified customer benchmarks:
- **Content savings:** 85% of current content spend (Rostrum eliminated entire $5K agency)
- **Agency savings:** 80% of current agency/freelancer spend (Fat Beats replaced $10K creative director)
- **Time savings:** 75% of staff hours × $40/hr industry average (Parlophone automated 40+ hrs)
- **Plan recommendation:** Pro ($99/mo) for ≤15 artists and <$8K combined spend; Partner ($5,000/mo) for larger operations
- **Net savings:** Gross savings minus Recoup plan cost
- **Break-even:** Recoup monthly cost ÷ daily gross savings
